01 / about

bass knows
no bounds

T3MPURA is a Toronto-based bass DJ and producer whose sound lives beyond genre lines. Rooted in dubstep, his sets move freely through hybrid trap, UK garage, UK bass, and bass house — guided by one thing: energy.

Having played the NXNE Music Festival and Artscape 2 in 2026, he keeps crowds locked in through constant momentum and carefully placed surprises, no matter what time it is in the night.

Known for creative mashups and edits that tap into nostalgia, his sets bridge the gap between underground bass culture and broader audiences — pulling in seasoned ravers and first-time listeners alike, with familiar moments flipped into something heavier, fresher, and unforgettable.
